# More predictable voice recording and clearer feedback

- Hands-free recording waits through a natural pause before sending. Uncertain
  turns end after three seconds of quiet; very short uncertain speech receives
  a retry prompt instead of remaining stuck or dispatching a guessed command.
- Switching briefly to Space and back restarts the silence window, preserving
  speech without inheriting an old stop timer. Agent questions remain answerable
  after a short-speech retry.
- The microphone shows its current status and offers Send during automatic
  recording. Old clicks, holds and failed flushes cannot affect a newer turn;
  keyboard capture failures are visible, and stale startup state cannot disable
  an otherwise-ready Space shortcut.
- Invalid assistant interpretations get one bounded repair attempt. Existing
  target, generation and supplied-answer checks still apply.
- Voice diagnostics retain bounded timing, completion confidence and recording
  reasons without audio or transcript content. The current voice audit replaces
  superseded reports, unused helpers are removed, and obsolete overlay QA is
  replaced by a focused history/context test.
- Release gates now include the paced native recording workflow and real
  Electron voice/history checks before publishing the installer and update feed.

Verification includes backend and renderer tests, native synthetic speech,
packaged inference, and a synthetic greeting through the configured live cloud
models. Physical microphone accuracy and audible playback quality still need
direct measurement. Voice remains turn-based: “Hey Vibe” does not interrupt
transcription, assistant work or playback. The installer remains unsigned.

Use **Check for update → Update → Restart** to install when your workspace is ready.
