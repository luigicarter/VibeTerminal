vibeTerminal 0.1.82 adds the optional voice/text Orchestrator and a redesigned dark workspace.

- Find current and saved conversations by title, inspect recent output, and relay exact instructions to selected sessions.
- Bring your own OpenRouter key and choose the orchestration model. Voice supports local “Hey Vibe” detection, cloud transcription/replies, and bundled offline error announcements.
- Browse and search saved conversation text progressively without loading whole histories into model context.
- Create projects under your Documents folder, use saved workspace setups, and stage handoffs between sessions.
- Use a balanced launcher grid, resize or fully collapse the workspace dock, and reorder sidebar projects with drag-and-drop or keyboard controls.
- Improve session identity, Gemini/runtime observations, pane placement, stale-target handling, cancellation and delivery acknowledgments.

The Orchestrator starts off and requires setup. Voice/cloud services use your own account. Unconfirmed agent input is staged instead of blindly submitted, and permission decisions remain yours.

Validation includes 207 targeted tests and isolated Electron tests for real terminal delivery, history navigation, project ordering, dock behavior and silent audio playback. Live provider behavior and physical microphone/speaker quality are not exhaustively validated.
