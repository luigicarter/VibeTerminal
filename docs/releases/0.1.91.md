# Hold Space to talk

- Voice is now push-to-talk. With voice on, hold the space bar while no terminal
  or text field is focused, speak, and release to send. The last two seconds
  before the press are included, so the first word is never lost. Taps under
  300 ms are ignored, and pressing Space during a spoken reply interrupts it.
- The "Hey Vibe" wake word is gone, along with the local keyword spotter, its
  native runtime, and its model files. Questions from the app are answered with
  the same hold. The mic pill is press-and-hold with the mouse.
- Spoken replies are more reliable: the recording no longer closes before you
  start talking, small differences in how the speech provider labels its audio
  no longer fall back to the offline voice, and Brain models with mandatory
  reasoning get a reply budget that fits, with a one-time retry when needed.
  Failures now say which stage failed.
- A malformed saved preferences list no longer breaks the Settings panel, and a
  stuck audio window is rebuilt on retry.

The fallback alert phrases are still the offline Windows voice, so a failed turn
sounds different from a successful one. Real-microphone behavior and the live
speech provider were not exercised by the automated checks; verify with a real
voice turn after updating. Details are in `docs/voice-push-to-talk.md` and
`docs/orchestrator-voice-deep-dive.md`.

Use Check for update, then Update and Restart to install.
